# Changelog

## 1.0.2

- Each of your characters has its own profile, bio, follows and notifications (an alt no longer shows your main's bio).
- Safer network: a message only counts as from someone when it really is them, and passed-on likes, dislikes and checks are limited.
- Colour and link codes are taken out of captions and comments.
- Mentioning the same person twice makes two proper links.
- Back returns to the same profile or picture; a picture opens scrolled to the top; the "..." menu closes when you scroll.
- Pictures use less CPU once they've loaded.
- Works side by side with 1.0.0: pictures, likes, comments and backgrounds look the same to both.

## 1.0.0

The first release: Instagram for your WoW character, on Retail and WoW Forever.

### Create
- You, in the picture, exactly as you look right now, transmog included.
- **Outfit**: take pieces off and put them back, or try on any item (drop it, shift-click a link, or type its ID).
- **People**: add your target or party members, up to 4 in a picture; right-drag to move anyone.
- **Pose**: 22 poses, Full / Waist up / Face, weapons out, and **Hold still** (frozen mid-move, like a photo).
- **Backdrop**: 150+ backgrounds: colours, your own two colours, moving ones (Disco, Aurora, Starfield, flowing Lava and Ocean), ground textures, the 27 talent tree paintings, book pages, the classic Dressing Rooms, 32 loading screens and 48 old world maps.
- **Filter**: 21 looks, from Golden hour to Fel, Blood Moon, Ghost and Vaporwave.
- **Motion**: Rainbow, Disco, Strobe, Glitch, Drunk, Dizzy, Earthquake, Hype, Underwater, Heartbeat, Spooky.
- **Overlay**: 44, from snow and fireworks to lightning, sunbeams, raining loot and murlocs.
- **Stickers**: 34; pick one and click where it goes, then drag, size and turn it.
- **Text** in 9 styles, including an Achievement Earned banner, Epic loot and Level up; long text shrinks to fit.
- **Frame**: Polaroid, Wanted poster, Film, Breaking news, Magazine cover, Boss fight and more.
- **Lens**: face filters on each person's head (ears, blush, glasses, crown, halo, a quest "!", a nameplate...), and body lenses (Tiny, Giant, Upside down, Spin, Float, or a sheep, frog, murloc... or Ragnaros).

### Share
- Realm, Guild and Following feeds; stories; likes (double-click), anonymous **dislikes**, comments, saves, notifications, profiles and follows.
- **Block** someone: their pictures, comments, likes and dislikes are hidden for you.
- A Realm feed that puts **friends** first, then people you follow, your guild and people you've **seen lately**; strangers' old pictures drop out.
- **Hashtags**, **@mentions** (with a notification), a **tag search** at the top of the wall, and **Search** for people (only those who have Wowstagram) and tags.
- **Guild only** pictures. On Retail a guild spans every realm and both factions.
- **The look**: every item in a picture, per person in group pictures; **Try this look** on yourself; **Save to computer** as a screenshot.
- Other people show as themselves when your game can see them, otherwise as a stand-in or a look-alike of their race (the badge says which).
- **Nothing made up lasts**: passed-on pictures, comments and likes are checked with their owner in the background; fakes vanish, and whoever made them up is shut out after a few.
- Built for busy realms: a few players answer each login, every sender is rate-limited, and old pictures make way for new ones (yours and your saved ones are always kept; friends' and followed people's have their own room).

### Also
- Fullscreen, window size, sounds, a chat line for likes and comments, a minimap button.
- `/wgram` opens it; `/wgram demo` brings in made-up people to try it all alone.
