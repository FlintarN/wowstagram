# Wowstagram

Instagram for your World of Warcraft character. Put yourself in the picture, change the outfit, strike a pose, add filters, stickers and face filters, and share it with your realm or your guild. Everyone with Wowstagram sees it in their feed, and can like it, comment on it and follow you.

![Retail](https://img.shields.io/badge/World%20of%20Warcraft-Retail-blue) ![WoW Forever](https://img.shields.io/badge/World%20of%20Warcraft-Forever-blue)

## Installation

1. Download the latest release.
2. Extract the folder into your game's `Interface/AddOns/` folder: `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/` for Retail, `World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/` for WoW Forever.
3. Make sure the folder is named `Wowstagram`.
4. `/reload` in game (or log in), then type `/wgram`.

## Slash Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/wgram` (or `/wowstagram`) | Open or close Wowstagram (also the camera button on the minimap) |
| `/wgram demo` | Demo people on or off: made-up people with their own pictures, to try it all alone |

## Create

You're in the picture, exactly as you look right now, transmog included. A tool rail next to the picture, like a phone camera's:

- **People**: add your target or party members, up to 4 in a picture. Right-drag anyone to move them.
- **Outfit**: take pieces off and put them back, or try on any item (drop it from your bags, shift-click a link, or type its ID).
- **Pose**: 22 poses, Full / Waist up / Face, weapons out, and **Hold still** (frozen mid-move, like a photo).
- **Backdrop**: 150+ backgrounds: colours, your own two colours, moving ones (Disco, Aurora, Starfield, flowing lava and water), ground textures, the classic talent tree paintings, book pages, the Dressing Rooms, loading screens and old world maps.
- **Filter**: 21 looks, from Golden hour to Fel, Blood Moon, Ghost and Vaporwave.
- **Motion**: Rainbow, Disco, Strobe, Glitch, Drunk, Dizzy, Earthquake, Hype, Underwater, Heartbeat, Spooky.
- **Overlay**: 44, from snow and fireworks to lightning, sunbeams, raining loot and murlocs.
- **Stickers**: raid markers, quest marks, faction crests, gold, murlocs and more. Pick one, click where it goes, then drag it, size it with the wheel and turn it with Shift + wheel.
- **Text** in 9 styles, including an Achievement Earned banner, Epic loot and Level up.
- **Frame**: Polaroid, Wanted poster, Film, Breaking news, Magazine cover, Boss fight and more.
- **Lens**: face filters on each person's head (cat or bunny ears, blush, glasses, crown, halo, a quest "!", a nameplate...) and body lenses (Tiny, Giant, Upside down, Spin, Float, or a sheep, frog, murloc... or Ragnaros).

Add a caption, choose **Guild only** if you like, and **Share**.

## Share

- **Feeds**: Realm, Guild and Following. The Realm feed puts your friends first, then people you follow, your guild and people you've seen lately.
- **Likes** (double-click a picture), anonymous **dislikes**, **comments**, **saves** and **notifications**.
- **Profiles** with a bio, **follows**, **#hashtags**, **@mentions** and **Search** for people and tags.
- **The look**: every item in a picture, with tooltips. **Try this look** puts it on you.
- **Save to computer**: any picture, as a clean screenshot.
- **Block** anyone: their pictures, comments, likes and dislikes disappear for you.

## How it works

A picture isn't an image. It's a short note: which appearances are worn, the pose, the backdrop and the fun on top. Everyone's game draws it again in 3D, so pictures cost almost nothing to send. Other people show as themselves when your game can see them, otherwise as a stand-in or a look-alike of their race (a small badge says which).

Pictures go to everyone with Wowstagram on your realm and in your guild (on Retail a guild spans every realm and both factions), or only to your guild. Nothing is stored on a server: each player's addon keeps what it has seen.

**Safe by design.** Only you can edit or delete your things, and nobody can like or comment in your name. Pictures, comments and likes passed on by others are checked with their owner in the background; anything made up disappears, and whoever made it up is shut out. Nobody's colour codes, textures or links work in captions, comments or bios.
